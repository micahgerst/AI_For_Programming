# From your exploration, what are two limitations of using a bare local model like this for real project work?
1. Limited resources. It only has available the model you downloaded, not a greater pool of data like a cloud hosted one.
2. Hallucinations. AI models are prone to hallucination, such as wrong NCBI accession numbers and fake packages.
3. Cannot upload files or additional context that could help the model.
4. Limited variation without setting temperature.
